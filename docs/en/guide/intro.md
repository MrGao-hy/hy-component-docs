## :book: Introduction

We are a small team that blends product thinking, UI aesthetics, and frontend magic into one cup of coffee.<br> During the day, we are constantly pulling and tugging between "usability" and "aesthetics"; at night, we still turn pixels and inspiration into stars together.

Thus, the HuaYue Component Library was born —<br> Over 70 meticulously crafted components, from the micro-animation of a button to the arrangement of full-page waterfall effects, can be assembled like LEGO; <br> Pre-configured for business scenarios: login, payment, marketing pop-ups, and more — ready-to-use, or easily customizable; <br> The theme system is as simple as changing skins — dark mode, vibrant, minimalist, just one click to infuse different souls into your application; <br> Cross-platform synchronization: WeChat Mini Program, H5, APP, "Write once, run everywhere" is our romantic declaration.<br>

We believe:<br> Technology only has warmth when it is ignited by passion; <br> Ecosystems only grow a soul when nurtured with respect.

Thank you —<br> Because of you, this path of exploration, <br> From a line of lonely code, has become a shining galaxy.
